Five services your clients will pay for

Each one grows out of work you already do: cloud, licensing, security and helpdesk. You already know where the manual work lives in every account.

Buyers value MSPs on recurring revenue, at roughly 6 to 8 times EBITDA (Channel Insider). The guidebook shows how to package each build as a project fee plus a monthly run-and-support agreement, so new revenue stays recurring.

AI agents and workflow automation

Automate intake, approvals, onboarding and first-line support. Patient intake and prior authorization for healthcare; loan, KYC and document review for financial firms.

System integrations and APIs

Connect accounting, CRM, line-of-business apps and cloud tools so data is entered once.

Client and customer portals

Secure, branded portals for documents, status, appointments and payments, built and run by you.

Custom line-of-business apps

Replace the spreadsheet a critical process depends on. A business that runs on its own software rarely switches providers.

Compliance and reporting tools

Audit trails, evidence collection and dashboards that turn your security work into proof clients can show regulators and insurers.
